# Search recipes

Use these when this agent needs to look something up. Replace the brackets. Keep queries
short — two to six words works best. Use whatever search you have.

## Is anyone already paying for this?

- `[market] agency pricing`
- `[competitor] pricing page`
- `[problem] retainer cost`
- `[market] case study results`
- `[market] "we hired an agency"`
- `[market] cost per lead`

Look for **money moving**. Not likes, not views.

## Is it crowded?

- `best [market] agencies 2026`
- `[market] agency reddit`
- `[competitor] vs`
- `[competitor] alternative`
- `[market] "everyone is doing"`
- `cold email [market] reply rates`

## What buyers complain about — the best material

- `[competitor] reviews`
- `[competitor] complaints reddit`
- `[competitor] alternative` — the reasons people search this **are** the gaps
- `[problem] "struggling with"`
- `[market] "not happy with" agency`
- `[market] in-house vs agency`

## Is demand growing, flat, or leaving?

- `[market] trends 2026`
- `[market] market size`
- `[market] hiring`
- `what changed in [market] 2026`

## Who to sell to

- `[role] [market] linkedin`
- `[market] companies hiring [role]`
- `[market] funding 2026`
- `[market] new [role]` — a new leader usually changes suppliers

## When you record a find

Keep three things: a short quote, the link, and the date you saw it.
Five strong finds beat thirty weak ones.

**Never** invent a quote, a review count or a number, and never repeat a competitor's own
marketing as if it were a fact.
